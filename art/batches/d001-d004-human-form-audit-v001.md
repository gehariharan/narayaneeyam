# D001–D004 human-form audit v001

**Status:** review-only audit; no artwork is approved by this report.

## Scope and checks

Visually checked all landscape candidate PNGs present under `artifacts/d001/` through
`artifacts/d004/`: 54 versioned candidates across 45 selected slokas and preserved
alternate versions. Contact sheets covered every candidate version; crowded or
ambiguous compositions were inspected at full size. Checks included limb and hand
counts, arm-to-shoulder attachment, face and torso coherence, fused or duplicated
people, hand ownership in overlapping poses, joint direction, and clipped limbs.

## Finding and correction

D001 S002 v001 packed several worldly-life figures into overlapping symbolic
vignettes. Although the intended gestures were discernible, hands and arms crossed
between figures and ownership was ambiguous at review size. It has been replaced in
the D001 comparison selection with the independently composed
`artifacts/d001/s002/landscape/candidate-v002.png`. The revised composition spaces
out the figures and gives each readable, attached arms. The v001 candidate remains
preserved for rollback.

## Other reviewed images

No other clear unintended extra-limb or fused-person defect was found in the selected
D001–D004 set or its preserved landscape alternates during this pass. D002 S002
intentionally depicts the Lord with four arms carrying conch, discus, mace, and lotus,
as required by `art/plans/d002.json`; this is divine iconography, not a human anatomy
error. The other people remain two-armed.

All candidates remain `needs-review`; approved masters and approval manifests were
not changed. Portraits were outside this audit because they remain paused.
