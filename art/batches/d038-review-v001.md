# Dasakam 38 comparison review

The Jarvis import fits the studio's content/plan/candidate separation and contains
10 selected landscapes, earlier candidate revisions, a first portrait candidate,
character sheets, source references and commentary. It is Dasakam 38, the birth
of Krishna, rather than Dasakam 40. Source text and the all-missing approval
manifest were imported unchanged; no stanza is approved by this review.

## Identity and style

The user delegated the character-continuity decision. Sheet c002 is accepted for
this comparison pass: Vasudeva's cream clothing, beard and topknot, Devaki's maroon
sari and braid, and infant Krishna's blue complexion, curls, yellow cloth and
peacock ornament share the D001/D002 vocabulary with an appropriate narrative age.
The imported sheet's light blue, shiny skin is not the traditional rendering target.
The new matte treatment uses deeper blue, dark contours, opaque pigment fields,
flat halos and simplified cloth. Character-decision records are in the bibles.

The ten original selected landscapes remain the Detailed painting comparison.
Their polished faces, light gradients and cinematic atmosphere are intentional
comparison evidence, not the series' traditional mural direction. The new
Traditional mural candidates use D001 S004 and D038 S001 as rendering anchors.
S002-S004 needed a second style pass because the first retained excessive modelling.

## Review scope and remaining checks

- Check one infant per scene and the four-armed iconography in S003-S006.
- S007 uses symbolic fading arm outlines around the same two-armed infant.
- S004 conveys Lakshmi through the heart/lotus motif; this is distinct from Devaki.
- S008 requires credible support under the infant, attached wrists and natural hands.
- S009 sleeping figures must read as resting, with released restraints and open doors.
- S010 keeps Adishesha protective, faces unobscured, and the infant sheltered.
- Tiny/occluded fingers and toes still require human editorial review; visual QC is
  not a guarantee of perfect anatomy. Close narrative crops are not portrait masters.
- The supplied reference images retain their original content and may contain marks;
  generated candidates must not introduce readable text, signatures or watermarks.

Every image remains a candidate. Portrait pairs, print-resolution review, sourced
text verification and explicit stanza approvals remain unfinished. The older
portrait candidate is preserved, not presented as an approved companion.
