# Series color and border review

User requested cross-chapter color and border continuity during D096 completion.
Visually compared D001 S004 traditional master and detailed v002, D002 S004
traditional v002 and detailed v001, D038 S004 traditional v003, and D096
traditional/detailed selections (all new D096 images were inspected). This is
a representative cross-chapter audit, not a claim that all older images have
identical treatment.

| Chapter/style | Observed treatment | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D001 traditional | Full-bleed, limited ochre/maroon/blue palette, strong flat contour | Primary baseline |
| D002 traditional | Full-bleed architecture, same earth palette, more green/rose accents | Close series fit |
| D001/D002 detailed | Full-bleed, much brighter gold and modelled skin | Intended alternative rendering; warmer and more saturated |
| D038 traditional | Four-sided floral frame, larger motifs, warm dark interiors | Border exception to baseline |
| D096 traditional | Four-sided maroon/ochre frame with small repeated flowers, flat earth palette | Palette close; border differs from baseline |
| D096 detailed | Usually top/bottom floral bands, bright sunset gold and modelled figures | Rendering comparison works; frame differs even within D096 pair |

Decision: use full-bleed D001/D002 as the border target for future production.
Do not introduce a new ornament or frame treatment per chapter. Preserve the
current comparison candidates; they are not final approved series masters.
Record D038/D096 border normalization as a separate versioned ImageGen
correction pass. Cropping borders away would remove composition and is not
an acceptable correction. No source reference image should be restyled.

Traditional color remains close enough for the comparison pass. The detailed
option intentionally has stronger highlights, but must retain blue identity,
maroon cloth and earth colors rather than introducing a new palette. Scenes
may need sea blue, foliage green or night darkness; identical global color
proportions would flatten the story and are not the goal.
