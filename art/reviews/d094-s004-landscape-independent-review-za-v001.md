His skin is muted rose-ochre, appropriately distinct from Bhattathiri's brown guide. Each is a tall, tiered, brightly highlighted gold form with repeated jewel-like bands; the central crowns rise conspicuously above the heads. The region is a focal, high-contrast part of Brahma's head cluster, readily noticeable at full size.
