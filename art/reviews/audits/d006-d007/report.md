## Findings

### F-D006-STYLE-01
- **Scope/category:** all inspected published D006 versions; severity moderate; confidence high (0.9).
- **Location:** global image treatment; normalized bbox [0,0,1,1].
- **Observed:** The set has dense engraved/granular texture and high-contrast bright gold ornament highlights compared with the series bible’s broad matte mineral fields and painted ochre. Several scenes also use heavy architectural framing, whereas the bible calls for full-bleed composition. This is rendering drift, not an anatomy defect.
- **Expected basis:** art/bible/series.json style description and frame_continuity: firm contours, broad matte fields, painted ochre instead of metallic gold, full bleed.
- **Repair direction:** If this batch is brought to production, regenerate affected scenes with the D001 matte references and explicitly suppress reflective gold, texture overlays, and heavy outer frames.

### F-D006-S007-V001-01
- **Scope/category:** scene fidelity; severity moderate; confidence high (0.9).
- **Location:** Virat garment/back, central figure; bbox [0.38,0.36,0.24,0.48].
- **Observed:** The visible alternate v001 lacks a clear Adharma/back mapping called for by the scene plan; the selected v002 adds a dark patterned back-facing shawl. Anatomy in v001 is otherwise coherent.
- **Expected basis:** art/plans/d006.json S007 scene brief requires Adharma mapped to the back and an opaque twilight garment.
- **Repair direction:** Keep v002 selected; v001 should remain visibly marked as the superseded scene-incomplete alternate.

### F-D006-S008-DETAIL-01
- **Scope/category:** inspection limitation; severity low; confidence high (0.95).
- **Location:** four small human groups surrounding Virat; distributed, approx bbox [0.02,0.17,0.96,0.72].
- **Observed:** At full image size, group figures are small; gross two-arm silhouettes and separation are visible, but fingers and some shoulder-to-wrist details are too small to clear individually. No extra or fused limb is evident at this resolution.
- **Expected basis:** art/plans/d006.json S008: four equally dignified ordinary-human groups with two arms each.
- **Repair direction:** For future publication approval, inspect a higher-resolution crop of each group; do not infer a pass for finger anatomy from the full-frame view.

### F-D007-STYLE-01
- **Scope/category:** all inspected published D007 versions; severity moderate; confidence high (0.95).
- **Location:** global image treatment; normalized bbox [0,0,1,1].
- **Observed:** Across the D007 set, dense surface texture and bright raised/reflective-looking gold ornament depart from the series bible’s matte mineral fields and painted ochre. This matches the existing D007 review note that rendering is more ornate and textured than the D001 anchors; this audit freshly observed the same issue.
- **Expected basis:** art/bible/series.json style/global_avoid; art/plans/d007.json scenes explicitly avoid glossy surfaces and metallic reflections.
- **Repair direction:** Regenerate candidates against D001 S004/S008 matte rendering references, requesting broad flat color fields and nonreflective ochre ornament.

### F-D007-S004-SCALE-01
- **Scope/category:** inspection limitation; severity low; confidence high (0.95).
- **Location:** Brahma in lower-left foreground; bbox approx [0.01,0.78,0.09,0.20].
- **Observed:** Brahma is deliberately very small in the wide landscape. The three visible heads are distinguishable, but the arms/hands are too small to verify four-arm anatomy at image resolution. No defect is asserted.
- **Expected basis:** art/plans/d007.json S004 requires a four-armed Brahma with three front-visible faces from four heads total.
- **Repair direction:** For approval review, inspect a readable crop or revise framing to make the witness large enough for arm verification.

## Recommendations for the no-mistakes image-review stage

1. Resolve the scene’s anatomy contract before counting: identify deities, ordinary people, intentional multiple appearances, and symbolic/cosmic forms; read the stanza plan and character bible first.
2. Count heads, arms, hands, legs, feet, eyes and fingers for each named person. Trace each visible limb from shoulder/hip through wrist/ankle; check ownership, attachment, joints, proportions, fusion and disconnection. Treat attributes as held only after inspecting the complete hand and forearm connection.
3. Compare crowd faces and bodies for accidental exact repeats within a single scene. Preserve intentional divine forms and recurring identities across different scenes.
4. Open every selected lead and visible alternate individually at native resolution. Zoom ambiguous overlaps and small crowds; mark uncertain when detail is too small or occluded rather than silently passing it.
5. Check face and eye coherence, floating attributes, accidental text/watermarks/logos, material style, framing, and scene fidelity against the plan and series bible. Track visible defects separately from interpretive or theological uncertainty.
6. Bind each review to the exact candidate version, read-only source path, full SHA-256, deployed key and live selection. Re-run when a published selection changes.
7. Describe findings with a stable ID, localized subject, normalized bounding box where useful, severity, confidence, and short repair direction. Seek counterevidence and inspect a close crop before making a confident anatomy claim.
