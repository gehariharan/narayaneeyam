## Source and story

intake/D014/004.jpg shows an ascetic and a bride seated together, and the plan uses it only as a marriage/blessing cue. The candidate shows the present blessing: Narayana seated on a lotus pedestal at left extends an open palm toward Kardama, who kneels at right in anjali. A bounded promise cloud between them holds a Devahuti bust (the wife), a row of nine lotus buds (the daughters), a child seated in a lotus (Kapila) and a radiant gateway with a path (liberation).

## Anatomy basis

Narayana has four attached arms by plan. Kardama has ordinary two-armed anatomy. The small Devahuti bust and infant in the promise cloud are framed symbolic vignettes.

- Limbs, hands, feet and fingers (pass): Narayana's upper viewer-left hand holds the chakra on a short stem, his lower viewer-left hand is in abhaya, his upper viewer-right hand holds the conch and his lower viewer-right palm is open toward Kardama; all have a thumb and fingers.
