#1

Swayambhu Manu's Son Uttanapada had two wives, Suneethi and Suruchi.
Suruchi was extremely dear to the King Uttanapada that he neglected his other wife Suneethi.
Because of this grief, Suneethi surrendered at the feet of Lord.

## 002.jpg

stanza: 2 | facebook file: D017/D017-2024-04-11-2.jpg | posted 2024-04-11 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#2
One day Dhruva, Suneethi's five year old son wished to sit on his father's lap after seeing his step brother Uttama seated. Suruchi scolded Dhruva harshly and pushed him away due to her jealousy which arises out of her lack of devotion for Lord.

## 003.jpg

stanza: 3 | facebook file: D017/D017-2024-04-11-3.jpg | posted 2024-04-11 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#3
Suneethi consoled her son and advised Dhruva that the one way to overcome the evil effects of one’s own past karma was to take shelter at Lord’s feet.

## 004.jpg

stanza: 4 | facebook file: D017/D017-2024-04-11-4.jpg | posted 2024-04-11 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#4
Dhruva left to forest and met Sage Narada on his way. Sage Narada gave Dwadashakshara Mantram and taught him how to perform devotion & how the Lord will look like. He reached Madhuvanam and performed penance for 5 months.

Mantram :
*OM NAMO BHAGAVATHE VAASUDEVAAYA*

## 005.jpg

stanza: 5 | facebook file: D017/D017-2024-04-11-5.jpg | posted 2024-04-11 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#5
Father Uttanapada was very worried that he was the reason for Dhruva to leave for penance at such a young age. Sage Naradha consoled the Father on reaching to his kingdom. Dhruva was totally devoted for 5 months in penance seeking the feet of Lord.

#6
On the fifth month, Dhruva controlled his breath perfectly and standing on only one leg meditated on the Parabrahman. Dhruva captured the Supreme God within his heart. Due to his controlled breath and becoming practically one in heaviness with Lord Vishnu, the universal breathing became choked. The demigods felt suffocated and took shelter of the Supreme Lord.
The Lord himself went to Madhuvanam and gave darshan to Dhruva appearing as seated on Garuda.

## 007.jpg

stanza: 7 | facebook file: D017/D017-2024-04-11-9.jpg | posted 2024-04-11 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#7
Soaked in bliss, Dhuruva was speechless, Lord touched the cheek of the boy with his conchshell (பாஞ்சஜன்யம்). Dhuruva sang the glory of the God, enlightened with the knowledge gained from the Lord.

## 008.jpg

stanza: 8 | facebook file: D017/D017-2024-04-11-10.jpg | posted 2024-04-11 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#8
Lord's Boon:
Pleased with the devotion and prayers of Dhruva, the Lord awarded the abode of Dhruvapadam.
It is surrounded by all solar systems, planets and stars and all luminaries in the sky. No one had ever ruled this planet.
The Lord told Dhruva that in future he will rule his father's kingdom for thirty six thousand years & he will not become old.
After enjoying the material life, he will go to the planet of the Lord and never again come back to the material world.

## 009.jpg

stanza: 9 | facebook file: D017/D017-2024-04-11-11.jpg | posted 2024-04-11 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#9

Dhruva returned to the Kingdom and ruled it peacefully with prosperity, after his father retired to the forest.

#10

Later, Uttama was killed by Yaksha and feeling enraged Dhruva raised a war against Yakshas, but with Swayambhu Manu's advice, he dropped the war. Yaksha king Kubera, pleased with his action, wanted to grant a boon, still Dhruva only wanted the boon of permanent devotion to Lord.
