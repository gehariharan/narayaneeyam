Dasakam 10

#1
By Bhagwan’s grace, with increased spiritual powers, Brahma started creating varieties of beings, those that grow on earth, non-moving like trees etc.,&  the moving, like animals birds etc., human beings and various divine beings. These serve as bodies for the jeevas to enter into.

#2
Brahma then created the five-fold effects of ignorance, which are wrong apprehension, egoism, attachment, anger and fear. Having created these extremely negative qualities and repenting for doing so, he resorted to meditation to Bhagwan for self purification.

#3
Brahma then from his mind created Sanaka, Sananda, Sanaatana Muni and Sanat Kumaras. Even though they were directed by Brahma to engage in the work of creation, they did not heed his words because they were highly devoted to Bhagwan.

#4
This made Brahma angry which he tried to control. That caused the birth of Mrida from between his eyebrows. Mrida at once cried out and told Brahma to provide him with names and abodes. Thus Mrida came to be known as Rudra.

#5
Brahma then prompted by Bhagwan, gave Rudra eleven names, forms, abodes and as many wives and humbly requested him to help in the creative process.

#6
Rudra’s creation consisted of fierce looking Rudras, which were filling up the three worlds. Brahma was frightened by them, and being prompted by Bhagwan, told Rudra not to create any more beings, and asked him to do penance for the benefit of the world.

#7
Brahma, who was intent on creation, from his own body created (the sages) Mareechi, Atri, Angiraas, Kratumuni, Pulaha, Pulastya, Bhrigu, Vasishtha, the Dakshas and also Shri Naarada, who is a devotee of Bhagwan.

#8
Brahma further created Dharmadeva, sage Kardama and then Saraswati. He was then overcome with desire for her, his own creation. However, prompted by Bhagwan, Sanaka Daksha and his other sons enlightened him and he gave up desire, being disillusioned.

#9
Brahma then brought out the Vedas and all the Puraanas and all the other branches of learning, from his four faces and taught them to his sons. He then found himself unable to further the process of creation and so he took refuge at Bhagwan’s lotus feet.

#10
Then prompted by Bhagwan and knowing the technique, Brahma, from the two sides of his body created the male and the female, named Manu and his wife Shataroopa. Through them he then multiplied the human race.
Bhattathiri prayed to Bhagwan to cure him of his disease.
