## 001.jpg

#1
Long ago, in the battle between the Devas and Asuras, some of the Asuras, even though killed by Bhagwan, were born again on the earth. Mother earth being very much tormented by their burden searched for refuge in Brahmaa and reached his abode, where the Devas had already gone.

## 002.jpg

#2
Goddess Earth was crushed by the immense weight of the evil minded Asuras and about to fall in the ocean of waters. Seeing the Earth in such a helpless state and also looking at the faces of the Devas, who had assembled there, Brahmaa meditated on Bhagwan.

## 003.jpg

#3
Brahma suggested that only Lord Hari could help at this point and hence led by Lord Shiva, Mother Earth, devas, Brahma and other gods reached the milky ocean and took refuge at the lotus feet of Bhagwan.

## 004.jpg

#4
All of them went to the Milky Ocean where a pleasant breeze was blowing. As they stood there with their minds meditating, the lotus born Brahmaa in his own heart heard Bhagwan’s words.

## 005.jpg

#5
Brahma said that Bhagwan is aware of the sad plight caused by asuras and hence will be born in Yadava clan with all his powers. The Devas will be born as rishis, wives of Devas will be born in earth to serve him. These words brought happiness and peace to all of them.

## 006.jpg

#6
In the famous city of Mathura, Vasudeva, son of King Surasena, ruling over Mathura city, married Devaki, the daughter of Devaka.

## 007.jpg

#7
Devaki's brother Kamsa took the couple on procession in the chariot, to honour them. At that time, Bhagwan's voice sounded that Kamsa will be killed by Devaki's eighth son. Out of fear, he took his sword and caught Devaki by hair and was about to kill her.

## 008.jpg

#8
Vasudeva pleaded  Kamsa to spare her life but those did not make any positive impact on Kamsa. After Vasudeva promised to hand over all his children, the moment they are born, Kamsa let Devaki free. 
When the first child was born, Vasudeva handed over the child to Kamsa. But he did not kill the child out of love.

## 009.jpg

#9
Sage Narada prompted by Bhagwan, advised him that he is an Asura and Yadavas were Devas, Hari the master of Maaya will be born in any of the birth to kill him. Kamsa then chased the yadavas and killed all the 6 children born to Vasudevan and Devaki.

## 010.jpg

#10
Bhagwan Adhisesha entered the womb of Devaki, when she concieved for seventh time. Bhagwan prompted Yoga Maaya to transfer the foetus to the womb of Rohini. Lord then entered the womb of Devaki at her eighth pregnancy. Devas and Gods praised Bhagwan in appreciation for his avathaaram as Sri Krishna to commence on earth sooner.
