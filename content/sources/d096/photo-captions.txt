## 001.jpg

#1
Bhagwan alone are Brahma perceptible. HE is the beginning letter Aa in the list of letters, Pranava mantra among mantras, Manu among kings, Bhrigu among Brahma rishis, Narada among Devarishis, Prahlada among asuras, Kamadhenu among the celestial cows, Garuda among birds, Ananthan among snakes, Ganga among rivers.

## 002.jpg

#2
Among persons devoted to the holy men, Bhagwan is Bali, among sacrifices Japayoga, among warriors Arjuna, among devotees HE is Uddhava. HE is the strength of the strong and grandeur of the majestic. There is no end to HIS manifested glories. All things brilliant and outstanding are Bhagwan.. HE is the life, HE is the nature, and there is nothing which HE is not in this universe.

## 003.jpg

#3
Non-attachment sprouts and matures gradually to people in the four Varnas and the aashramas, who perform their duties, as in the Vedas, with devotion and dedication to Bhagwan. When they are fully non-attached, they give up these duties and attain the true knowledge of HIM which is the experience that HE is omnipresent  & HE is the one responsible for every cause, yet HE is beyond all of them rightly termed as Paramathma.

## 004.jpg

#4
Jnana, Karma and Bhakti are the three different yogic paths to reach Bhagwan. 
Those who are fully dispassionate towards everything in life, the path of Jnana yoga suits best. For those who possess worldly attachments, the path of Karma yoga is best to follow. To those who are neither intensely attached nor intensely dispassionate and also prefer experiencing Bhagwan, the path of Bhaakti is prescribed.

## 005.jpg

#5
In this world, we follow Jnana or Bhakti path depending on our good karma. Whether in heaven or even hell, the man crosses the samsara ocean using this body as the boat. Hence Bhattathiri requested Bhagwan to give him, the Guru who will captain the boat and Bhagwan by being the wind, facilitating the boat to cross the ocean of samsara and reach HIM.

## 006.jpg

#6
People who prefer Jnana Yoga to reach Bhagwan follow Vedic ways but which is difficult to attain & then after trying through different birth cycles, they attain moksha.
Karma Yoga is far fetched from the ultimate goal. Certainly the path of devotion is sweet from the very beginning and quickly leads a devotee to Bhagwan. Bhattathiri prayed for this Bhakti to grow more and more in him.

## 007.jpg

#7
Sage Vyasa too has asked to avoid trying moksha through Jnana yoga. He has mentioned that a person, who seeks meaning of Brahma thathvam, surrender at the feet of Bhagwan, moksha is easily attainable. In Dhyana yoga too, there could exist hurdles equally like Jnana Yoga, due to distractions. But with practice and mercy of Bhagwan, one can attain moksha.

## 008.jpg

#8
Bhattathiri mentioned his disinterest in the path of Karma yoga & firmly places faith in hearing to Bhagwan’s narrations. Even then, if he is not able to give up desires, he will indulge in them with the awareness of their harmfulness and again with determination fixing his mind in Bhagwan, will strengthen his devotion. When Bhagwan comes in to one's heart, all the unwanted desires get destroyed on its own.

## 009.jpg

#9
A  Brahmin who had earned and accumulated money by inappropriate means happened to lose it all.. He lost attachment to wealth and property  but still people 
 harassed him. Considering that the trouble experienced by him was due to his own mind and not the people or time or actions or planets position, he attained peace and surrendered to Bhagwan and eventually attained moksha. Bhattathiri requested Bhagwan to bless him with such a peaceful mind.

## 010.jpg

#10
King Pururavas, son of Ilaa, long ago was deeply infatuated by Urvashi and enjoyed her company for long. Later he realised that pleasure obtained from women is wretched one and  became fully devoted to Bhagwan & moved about freely as one liberated. Bhattathiri worshipped to Bhagwan to uproot all his desires, make him the best of Bhagwan’s devotees and free him  of all his diseases.
