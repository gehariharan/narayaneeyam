Stotra Nidhi source excerpt — Narayaneeyam Dasakam 48, stanza 4
Retrieved: 2026-11-15

Devanagari page: https://stotranidhi.com/hi/narayaneeyam-dasakam-48-in-sanskrit/
भिया प्रियालोकमुपात्तवाससं
पुरो निरीक्ष्यापि मदान्धचेतसौ ।
इमौ भवद्भक्त्युपशान्तिसिद्धये
मुनिर्जगौ शान्तिमृते कुतः सुखम् ॥ ४८-४ ॥

English (IAST) page: https://stotranidhi.com/en/narayaneeyam-dasakam-48-in-english/
bhiyā priyālokamupāttavāsasaṃ
puro nirīkṣyāpi madāndhacetasau |
imau bhavadbhaktyupaśāntisiddhaye
munirjagau śāntimṛte kutaḥ sukham || 48-4 ||

Editorial note: the source's romanization line has “madāndhacetasau”; the project extended-vowel spelling normalizes this as “madāndhacētasau”. The earlier project string “madāndhacētāsau” lengthened the following a and did not match either the source's Roman line or the Devanagari. Only that transcription error is corrected; no translation or sourced Sanskrit is changed.
