## 001.jpg

#1
Bhattathiri mentions that the most fortunate and blessed people are those who engage in Bhagwan’s worship and do HIS namasankeerthanam, talk of HIS divine stories and continuously soak themselves in bliss.

## 002.jpg

#2
Bhattathiri’s mind was not inclined to revel in the joy of worshipping at Bhagwan’s lotus feet as he was tormented by the  disease.  He requested Bhagwan to reduce the intensity of his pain so that he can sit in a corner and sing in praise of Bhagwan peacefully.

## 003.jpg

#3
When Bhagwan’s grace is present, is there anything in this world which man cannot attain? The curing of Bhattathiri’s disease is just a very insignificant matter for  Bhagwan. There are many devotees who having been liberated from sufferings and are moving about freely without any attachment.

## 004.jpg

#4
Bhattathiri recollected, how Narada and other divine sages attained supreme Bliss by chanting Bhagwan’s name and meditating upon HIM.

## 005.jpg

#5
Bhattathiri wished his devotion to grow intense, so that all his sufferings may automatically subside. And he does not have the slightest doubt about this. For if it were not so, the words of sage Vyasa, Bhagwan and the Vedas would prove to be untrue.

## 006.jpg

#6
 Devotion to Bhagwan is sweet right from the beginning because of Bhagwan’s glorious attributes. When such devotion becomes intense, it removes all sorrows of the devotee. At the final stage of devotion, the devotee experiences pure absolute Bliss coupled with knowledge of the Self.

## 007.jpg

#7
Bhattathiri pleads Bhagwan to be graceful to remove all his afflictions so that his two feet will reach Bhagwan’s temple, his hands to worship Bhagwan, his eyes to see the enchanting form,  his nose to enjoy the fragrance of the Tulsi leaves.

## 008.jpg

#8
As Bhattathiri’s mind was agitated due to mental and physical afflictions, he wished  Bhagwan’s beautiful form to manifest which will excite him with supreme devotion causing horripilation all over the body and tears flowing in ecstasy and his endless sorrows will disappear.

## 009.jpg

#9
Bhattathiri questions Bhagwan will it not bring disrepute when people who are being indifferent to Bhagwan are leading a happy life while he being an ardent devotee is undergoing sufferings. He pleads Bhagwan to eradicate his diseases and soon make him one of HIS foremost devotees.

## 010.jpg

#10
So repeatedly he requested for the removal of physical pain, so that he could spend all his life singing Bhagwan’s glory, worship HIM, prostrate at Bhagwan’s feet and become best of all HIS devotees. Lord Guruvayoorappan acknowledged his request immediately.
