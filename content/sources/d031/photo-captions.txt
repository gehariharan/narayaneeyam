## 001.jpg

#1 
Mahabali attracted by Bhagwan’s glorious majestic sight , did the welcoming rituals and offered him seat. He then requested Vamana with folded hands as to what he desires to have, wealth or house or food or land or all the above.

## 002.jpg

#2
Vamana, hearing Bali's words was moved with compassion, praised the asura clan with sweet words and with intention of removing his pride, asked for three steps of land, measured with his foot.

## 003.jpg

#3
Mahabali was taken aback by this and asked Vamana, why he is asking so less, when Mahabali who is conqueror of three worlds can even give him, the entire earth. Thus his pride got exposed.

## 004.jpg

#4
Vamana replied that one who is not satisfied with three steps of land, will not be satisfied with anything more. Bali was about to pour water and confirm his giving, when Guru Sukracharya objected to the gifting and said that it is Bhagwan who has come in the Brahmin form.

## 005.jpg

#5
 He told Shukracharya that if Bhagwan Himself has come, asking for land to Him, then he will definitely give. Shukracharya cursed Mahabali for refusing to listen to him. Mahabali's wife Vindhyaavali took the pot of water to pour and confirm the gift.

## 006.jpg

#6
 Mahabali gave everything to Bhagwan as per his promise without any hesitation and doubt. As the water was poured, divine sages and Gods showered flowers on Bhagwan. Vamana immediately grew to a giant size spanning the entire cosmos.

## 007.jpg

#7
 Bhagwan’s  feet  touched satyalokham, Brahma's abode. Brahma washed Bhagwan's feet with water in his kamandalam, which then fell as Akaasha Ganga purifying the entire universe. Gandharvas danced in ecstasy and the great devotee of Bhagwan, the Jaambhavan went around the world beating his drum.

## 008.jpg

#8
The Asuras started to fight with the Devas without Bali's permission. They were defeated by Bhagwan’s representatives who had come there and were fighting on the Deva's side. Bali told them that Bhagwan in the form of Trivikrama was standing before them by whose grace they had won earlier, and was now against them. So it was no use to fight. At this the Asuras went away to Paataala lokam.

## 010.jpg

#9
Garuda tied Bali with ropes and brought him before Bhagwan. Bhagwan questioned Bali where he can keep his third step. Bali with all humility, requested Bhagwan to keep the foot on his head to measure the third step. Grandfather Prahalad came to Bali's side and praised him with hymns at his humble act.

## 011.jpg

#10
Bhagwan happy with Mahabali, told him that all this was done only to destroy his pride. He will get to rule Suutalam world which is more superior than even Indraloka. He would attain the position of Indra also. With the help of Brahmins, Bhagwan helped the Vishavajit yagnya of Mahabali to get completed.
