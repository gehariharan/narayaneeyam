Dasakam 9

#1
Brahma sitting on the full bloomed lotus emerging from Bhagwan’s lotus-like navel, and wondering about its origin looked all around by turning his face in all the directions. He was thus endowed with four faces and lotus-like eight beautiful eyes.

#2
Brahma found himself to be all alone in the huge lotus which was tossing about in the vast ocean of causal waters. He saw the stem supporting the lotus but was unable to see Bhagwan’s body and wondered about the source of the lotus.

#3
Brahma who was of mighty intellect, decided that there must definitely be some source of this lotus. Using his yogic power he descended through the hollow of the lotus stem to look for the cause. He, however could not see Bhagwan’s most enchanting form.

#4
Brahma spent a hundred divine years strenuously searching through all the orifices of the lotus stem but he could not see anything. He returned to the lotus and sat calmly with one pointed concentration and resorted to deep samaadhi solely desirous of Bhagwan’s grace.

#5
The lotus born Brahma was absorbed in undisturbed samaadhi for a hundred divine years.The knowledge of Reality then dawned on him. He then had the vision of Bhagwan’s wonderful form which humans cannot easily see, resting on a part of Aadishesha's body. So he was full of happiness and contentment.

#6
Bhattathiri worshipped that divine form of Bhagwan, with a brilliant colour like a blue lily, sparkling like the Kalaya flower, decked with golden crown, bracelets, necklaces and shoulder ornaments, wearing yellow coloured silk garment, gold studded waist band and shining Kausthubh jewel round the neck.

#7
 Brahma felt it is his good fortune that Bhagwan have revealed HIS sacred form to him. Brahma wished to make his intelligence capable of creating the world. Brahma praised Bhagwan for the innumerable excellences and pleased to be merciful to protect him.

#8
Bhagwan blessed Brahma to be endowed with unending capability and expertise in the creation of the three worlds & his devotion to excel. Bhagwan guided to do penance again by which he will accomplish everything. So saying Bhagwan made Brahma's mind immensely happy.

#9
With tapas  for another 100 divine years, Brahma gathered spiritual and mental powers to start creation of three worlds and other beings. The Lotus swayed in the casual waters and he drank wind and water.

#10
Brahma, then, by Bhagwan’s grace created the three worlds out of that lotus itself and got engaged in creating the various species of beings. Bhattathiri requested Bhagwan to bestow on him, mercy and cure his ailments. Bhagwan acknowledged his request immediately.
