Dasakam 13

#1
Sage Naradha who is skillfully diplomatic in furthering Bhagwan’s avatara purposes, approached Hiranyaksha, who was running about searching for Bhagwan in the cosmic waters which barely reached his knees. Naradha calmly spoke to him, praising him and undermining Bhagwan’s prowess.

#2
Naradha told that decietful  Vishnu was stealing away HIS own earth. Hiranyaksha fierce with anger roared big going in search and spotted  Bhagwan rising up the waters with the earth.

#3
Hurling abuses at the Lord Varaha for its look of a wild boar, Hiranyaksha got ready to fight. Bhagwan lowered down the trembling Goddess Earth carefully from his tusk on to the sea floor and got ready to fight back.

#4
As Hiranyaksha was armed with mace, Lord Varaha too sported a mace and was playfully fighting with him. As the maces struck with each other, with huge sound, host of Gods assembled in the sky to witness the fight. Brahma cautioned Lord Varaha to kill Hiranyaksha before night fall. 

#5
During the fight, Hiranyaksha knocked down Lord Varaha’s mace. Smiling at this action, Bhagwan invoked the Sudarshan Chakra which is well known for slaying of the race of the Asuras. Bhagwan looked magnificent as the invoked discus placed itself in HIS hands.

#6
Angered by rage, Hiranyaksha threw a trident on Lord Varaha, which was again broken down by Sudarshan chakra. Hiranyaksha hit  Lord Varaha with his fist and with Maya tried many feats of warfare only to lose all before Bhagwan, who is above all maya.

#7
The illusions created by asura were all nullified by Lord Varaha's discus. He then resorted to heavy blows with his mighty fists when Lord Varaha, gave a deathly blow with his big toe at the base of the asura's ear.

#8
That gigantic Asura crushed by the powerful blow of Lord Varaha, fell down with blood flowing out of his mouth. Sages and Gods were delighted and sang hyms in appreciation of  Varaha Murthy brimming with joy.

#9
Bhagwan’s skin are the Vedic Mantras, hair are the 'kusha' grass, eyes are the ghee, feet are the four sacrificing priests, face is 'sruk',( the ladle for sacrifice) and  stomach is the 'Ida' (the vessel which holds the sacrificial ingredients), tongue is the soma pot, ears are the chamasas (the vessel which holds the sacrificial remnants) and  virility is soma, neck are the sacrifices called Upasat, (the subsidiary rites) and the entire body is the embodiment of the holy Yagna.

#10
Pleased with the hyms on his countless glory, Bhagwan concluded purpose ofHIS avatar and retired to Vaikunta. Bhattathiri requested Lord Guruvayoorappan to be pleased with him, and help him overcome his ailments. Bhagwan acknowledged his request immediately by nodding  head.
