## 001.jpg

#1
The world’s knowledge, faith, actions, dwellings, joys, and foods differ through the three qualities. What is centered on You and Your service is described as established beyond those qualities; by worshipping You, may I quickly attain that state.

## 002.jpg

#2
May I keep my mind on You, make every action an offering, and seek the sacred places served by Your devotees. Free of contempt, rivalry, and envy, may I honor You continually in every being, seeing a thief, a Brahmin, and animals with equal regard.

## 003.jpg

#3
While Your presence is not yet clearly seen in all things, let me worship in this way; through the insight of oneness may I become filled with You and move through life accordingly. Since this path of devotion is not to be abandoned, Lord, grant me the beautiful way of devotion with all my being.

## 004.jpg

#4
To strengthen this devotion, I need health and a long life; may Your feet be served as a remedy, like milk. Mārkaṇḍeya, once said by an astrologer to have only twelve years, served You for a year, and Your attendants drove Death away.

## 005.jpg

#5
Mārkaṇḍeya lived long and remained devoted to You, practicing austerity on the Pushpabhadrā for six manvantaras with incomparable joy. Indra, the seventh, sent celestial women, wind, and Kāma to distract him, but the heat of his yoga overcame their attempt; who could defeat one devoted to You?

## 006.jpg

#6
You, known as Nārāyaṇa and companion of Nara, joyfully came to Mārkaṇḍeya. Though offered many boons, he declined them, content in devotion; he chose instead to see Your māyā out of wonder, though he knew nothing of its suffering.

## 007.jpg

#7
After You departed, storms poured water from the clouds and the world sank beneath the churning seven oceans; Mārkaṇḍeya wandered helplessly through the waters for countless years. In the distance he saw a wondrous infant, dark blue like You, resting on a banyan leaf with its toes near its lotus mouth.

## 008.jpg

#8
Thrilled, the sage hurried toward You to touch You, but entered within through the infant’s breath and saw the whole expanse of worlds there. Cast out again by the breath and met by Your loving glance, he reached to embrace You; You disappeared, and he found himself back in his hermitage as before.

## 009.jpg

#9
Shiva, the foe of Tripura, came there with Gauri, wishing to see Your beloved devotee. He granted Mārkaṇḍeya attainments including freedom from old age and death, then departed; because even Shiva delights in service to You, it became clear that You embody the three divine forms and govern all.

## 010.jpg

#10
Above the three worlds and the abodes of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva shines Vaikuṇṭha, untouched by transformations of māyā. There You abide as pure being in the causal waters and among the cowherd folk, the nondual reality of existence and consciousness; Lord of Guruvayur, protect me from every illness.
