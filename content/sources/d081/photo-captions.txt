#5
The target which was a reflection of fish mark in water could not be hit by even Arjuna, but Krishna hit it and married the daughter of King of Madra, Lakshmanai. HE had eight wives married in similar manner known as the Ashtabharya : Rukmini, Satyabhaama, Jambavati, Kaalindi, Mitravinda, Nagnajiti, Bhadra, and Lakshmanai.
Krishna then came to know about the misdeeds of Narakasuran, son of Bhooma Devi, from Indra's words.

#7
The five faced asura, Muran attacked Krishna from the middle of the forest & was at once made headless by Krishna’s Sudharshana chakra cutting off his five heads. Then Narakasura gave a tough fight with his regiment of mighty four tusked elephants. After a long fight, Krishna cut his head and made him cross the ocean of samsara, saving from the suffering of hell.

Krishna went to Devaloka to return the earrings of Athithi which Narakasuran had taken away. Indra and other Devas honoured Krishna and Sathyabhama.

#10
The great sage Narada heard that Krishna had married sixteen thousand wives after HE had killed the demon Narakasura. Astonished that Bhagwan Krishna had expanded HIMSELF into sixteen thousand forms and married these wives simultaneously in different palaces, Narada desired to see these pastimes and set out to visit Krishna’s different homes in Dwaraka.

Bhattathiri prayed to Bhagwan to save him from his afflictions.
