Dasakam 12

#1
Swayambhu Manu, Son of Brahma started creating the species on earth. Suddenly he noticed that Earth was floating in water, even though there was no pralaya. Immediately he went to satyaloka, along with other sages and surrendered to Brahma who remains blissful  on the Lotus feet of Bhagwan.

#2
Swayambu Manu requested Brahma to give a place to put the species created by him, as the earth was sinking in the water. Brahma contemplated and surrendered at the lotus feet of Bhagwan. 

#3
Brahma took refuge at Bhagwan’s lotus feet saying that HE had earlier also drunk the waters and that the earth was still submerging and he did not know what to do. From Brahma’s nostrils, Bhagwan emerged in the form of a child boar.

#4
At first the size of the boar was like the size of a thumb, then it gradually grew to the size of an elephant. Brahma and his sons were wonder struck seeing the huge form in the sky. 

#5
Brahma was wondering as to who the glorious boar was which had come out from his nostrils. As he was trying to reflect if it was the work of the Invincible Maya, Bhagwan in the form of the boar became the size of a mountain and roared fiercely.

#6
The fierce roaring sound reached the Jana, Tapa and Satya lokas and the sages began to sing in praise of Bhagwan in the form of Varaha murthy. The pleased Bhagwan roared once again happily and jumped in to the ocean, searching for the Goddess Earth. 

#7
Lord Varaha had tail lifted up with reddish and black hair standing erect, had a fierce looking nose, pointed down and rolling eyes. With face slightly bent so as to bless the praising sages, Varaha broke through the clouds and jumped in to the ocean.

#8
Lord Varaha entered the insides of the waters where the crocodiles and whales were whirling around and which was full of turbulent waves. The inhabitants of Rasatala shook with fear as with a fierce roar Lord Varaha searched for the earth.

#9
Then seeing the earth concealed by the Asura at the bottom of the Rasatala, Lord Varaha lifted it up with the tip of the tusk of the boar. This was like a mere sport for Lord Varaha.

#10
The gigantic earth looked like a blade of grass stuck to the tip of the tooth of Lord Varaha.
Bhattathiri requested Lord Guruvayoorappan, who with his Maya took the form of Varaha and saved the Earth from sinking in ocean, to eradicate his ailments.
