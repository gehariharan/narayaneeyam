#1
Satraajit, a yadhava, prayed to Sun-god and got Syamantaka Mani, a priceless jewel as gift. Krishna asked Satraajit to give that jewel. Reasons for asking could have been many, but one main reason could be the strategy to marry his daughter Sathyabhama, who was in love with Krishna.

## 002.jpg

stanza: 2 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-2.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#2
Narrow minded Satraajit did not give it to Krishna, instead gave it to his brother Prasena. When he wore it around his neck and went for hunting, a lion thinking the jewel is flesh, attacked and killed him and took it. Jaambavaan, King of all animals, killed the lion and took the jewel and gifted it to his child.

## 003.jpg

stanza: 3 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-3.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#3
Satraajit announced that Krishna has stolen the jewel and people believed it too. A small fault in conduct, if found in good people, everyone will keep talking about it with great interest. Krishna knew everything that has happened to the jewel but still HE went in search of it. HE came across Prasena & the lion lying dead & were led to Jaambavaan’s cave.

## 004.jpg

stanza: 4 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-4.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#4
Due to old age, Jaambavaan failed to recognise Krishna and exclaimed that no one can win him, who has Mukundan as the saviour. With prayers to Lord Ram, he did pooja for Krishna with the blow of fists for almost 21 days.

## 005.jpg

stanza: 5 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-5.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#5
Later Jaambavaan recognised that the person whom he fought with is Sri Krishna, he gave his beautiful daughter & Syamantaka mani to Krishna. Bhagwan accepted and immediately returned the jewel to Satraajit.

## 006.jpg

stanza: 6 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-6.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#6
Satraajit felt ashamed that he had accused Krishna wrongly. He had only verbally promised that he will give his daughter in marriage to someone. But after this incident, he gave his beautiful eyed daughter Satyabhama & Syamantaka mani to Krishna. Having received the stree ratnam Sathyabhama itself, Krishna was happy and hence returned the jewel to Satraajit himself.

## 007.jpg

stanza: 7 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-7.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#7
Krishna was having a delightful time with Satyabhama. News of Kunti's sons burnt in wax palace reached Krishna and HE immediately left to Hastinapur. With the promptings of Akrura and Kritivermaa, Shatadanva killed Satraajit and took away the Syamantaka mani.

## 008.jpg

stanza: 8 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-8.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#8
Hearing about the father's death, Satyabhama was struck with grief and she reached Hastinapur. Krishna immediately killed Shatadanva and pleased Satyabhama. Balaram went to Mithila and there, he trained Duryodhana in the art of wielding the mace.

## 009.jpg

stanza: 9 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-9.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#9
Akrurar, extremely devoted to Krishna, had prompted the killing of Satraajit, due to his love for Krishna in order to avenge for the blame, Satrajit put on Krishna initially. Krishna did not take the jewel from Akrurar but let him keep the jewel himself. Krishna wanted to bring prosperity to Akrura and hence did not recover the jewel and blessed him.

## 010.jpg

stanza: 10 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-10.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#10
Akrurar was humbled by the magnanimity shown by Krishna to him. How could there be an evil thought in his mind (of killing Satraajit and getting the jewel)? He was bloated with the vanity of having self knowledge and self control. Bhagwan brought this about in order to curb this.

## 011.jpg

stanza: 11 | facebook file: D080/D080-2026-11-26-11.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#11
Still once again, Akrurar fled in fear along with Kritivermaa. Krishna brought Akrurar back along with the jewel and showed it to Balaram and others. Krishna gave the jewel to Akrurar, the one who does virtuous deeds.
Bhattathiri worshipped Bhagwan to protect him from his ailments.
