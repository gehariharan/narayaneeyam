#1
Bhattathiri wished to let go the attachment to his relatives which is only by the grace of Bhagwan. With mind fixed completely in Bhagwan, whatever he saw around are all nothing but an expression of maya. As long as one possesses attachment to the self, there will be some do's/don'ts & good/bad. These will not apply to a person whose mind is absorbed in Bhagwan and who posses a balanced mind.

#2
There are countless beings who keep satisfying their hunger and thirst. Taking birth as human being is the best of all. Such a human being becomes friend and enemy for himself. Bhattathiri wished to fix mind on Bhagwan and learn to solve his miseries of birth/death cycle through that, becomes friend to his soul and one who differs from this becomes enemy to his own soul.

## 005.jpg

stanza: 5 | facebook file: D093/D093-2026-11-26-5.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#5
Bhattathiri wished that he should not be like the PIGEON who for the love of its off springs was killed by the hunter, along with them. Like the PYTHON he may eat whatever is available and also bear hunger when nothing is available. Let him be deep and serene like the OCEAN. Let him not fall a prey to the lust of women like a MOTH to fire. Let him seek the essence of things like the HONEY BEE and unlike it, let him not perish in accumulating wealth.

## 006.jpg

stanza: 6 | facebook file: D093/D093-2026-11-26-6.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#6
Bhattathiri wished not to be led to captivity by women as an ELEPHANT is to a female. Let him not earn wealth in plenty because it is taken away by others, like the honey gatherers. Let him not be attracted into a snare by sweet words, as a DEER is by vulgar music. Let him not be too attached to food like a FISH is to a bait. Like PINGALAA, he wished to sleep soundly without a care. May he not be killed by others like the KURARA BIRD for having to guard the meat it was carrying.

#7
Bhattathiri wished to be like a happy and carefree CHILD. Bhattathiri wanted to avoid unnecessary talks and arguments like a solitary BANGLE to have peace in life. He wants to stay focused on Bhagwan like a person who aims his ARROW does not get distracted by the music of the instruments announcing King's arrival. Just like how SNAKES live in the hole made by the mouse, Bhattathiri wanted to stay happily, peacefully and unattached in house built by others.

## 008.jpg

#8
The SPIDER is a teacher in the sense, that, Bhattathiri learnt from it that the Universe created by Bhagwan, is absorbed into Bhagwan, by Bhagwan alone. From the BEETLE, he got the lesson that meditating on confers Bhagwan’s form. Finally, to Bhattathiri, his physical BODY which becomes ashes or feast to the worms in the end, suffering with the disease, was itself serving as the best Guru.

## 009.jpg

stanza: 9 | facebook file: D093/D093-2026-11-26-9.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#9
Bhattathiri wished to Bhagwan to get rid of him from helplessly getting attached to house, wealth, wife etc., as that makes to forget Bhagwan’s lotus feet. In the end this body is fit only for fire or dogs. Even now, while living, it is dragged hither and thither by senses of sight, hearing, smell, touch, taste. There is no one to take us to Bhagwan’s lotus feet.

## 010.jpg

stanza: 10 | facebook file: D093/D093-2026-11-26-10.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#10
Bhattathiri pleads to Bhagwan if obsession to body sense is unavoidable, then to help him get rid of all his ailments and endow him with unshakable firm devotion to Bhagwan’s lotus feet. He has received the physical form of Brahmin by good Karma in his previous births. Hence he requests to protect him from sensous pleasures and help him surrender only to Bhagwan’s lotus feet.
