Dasakam 44

#1
Vasudeva wanted to perform naming ritual for the baby. So at his request, Garga Muni, expert in  shastras visited Nandagopan's house.

#2
Nandagopan was very delighted and he honoured the greatest of all the sages Garga Muni and requested the sage to perform the rites.

#3
Garga Muni told Nandagopan since he is Acharyar for Yadhu vamsham, the naming ceremony should be conducted secretly. Saying so, with horripilation over his body he performed the naming ceremony.

#4
Garg Muni was apprehended how to name this child? 
Bhagwan has thousands of names or rather endless names. May be thinking like this, Garga Muni performed Bhagwan’s naming in great secrecy.

#5
Krish meant existence and Na meant the connection between the existence and absolute bliss form. Garga Muni declared the name of the baby as KRISHNA. Krishna also meant the one who removes the sin of all.

#6
Garga Muni also gave Bhagwan other different names like Vaasudeva. Then he gave the name (Bala Raama) Raama to Bhagwan’s elder brother. 
Garga Muni indicated that the baby has superhuman powers but he did not fully reveal the real identity as Bhagwan Himself.

#7
Garga Muni told Nandagopan that whoever is devoted to his son Krishna will never suffer and whoever troubles your son will perish.
Thus Garga Muni described Bhagwan’s glory and greatness.

#8
 Garga Muni said Bhagwan will conquer many Asuras and will take his own people to the realms of purity. Nandagopan will be hearing about his pure fame. Thus Garga Muni described Bhagwan’s greatness


#9
Garga Muni told  Vasudeva that all his sufferings will come to an end only through this child. Without mentioning that his son is Lord Hari himself, he asked Vasudeva to keep faith on the child. 

#10
After Garga Muni’s depature, Nandagopan and all gopas were very delighted and looked after Krishna dearly . Bhattathiri prayed to Bhagwan, to remove his afflictions with his mercy.
