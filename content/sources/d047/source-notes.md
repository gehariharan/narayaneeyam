# Dasakam 47 source notes

Canonical Devanagari and IAST are transcribed without editorial rewriting from Stotra Nidhi, Dasakam 47, numbered 47-1 through 47-10.

- Devanagari: https://stotranidhi.com/hi/narayaneeyam-dasakam-47-in-sanskrit/
- IAST: https://stotranidhi.com/en/narayaneeyam-dasakam-47-in-english/
- Numbered captions and scene photos: `intake/D047/`, copied byte-for-byte after SHA-256 checks against the D047 readiness record.
- Photo 005 caption says “cat,” while canonical stanza 5 and the photo depict monkeys. The plan follows the canonical stanza.
- Photo 009 depicts Yashoda with Krishna although the stanza and caption place her away and Krishna eating butter from the mortar hole.
- Photo 010 is a Damodara Lila title/cover poster, not an illustration of the devas praising Krishna in stanza 10. The plan derives the scene from canonical text.
- Photos 011 and 012 are Tamil text pages, retained as source material but excluded from scene mapping.
- No vetted English translation is supplied. The chapter keeps English meaning fields empty and preserves supplied captions verbatim as commentary.
