#1
Krishna was engaged in playing with the gopas and in Pralambasura vadham.At that time, cows wandered deep in to the forest and reached another forest called Aishika.

## 002.jpg

stanza: 2 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-2.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#2
The Cows which have never been exposed to heat in Vrindavan, now experienced an intense heat in this forest. The separation from Krishna and the summer heat made them thirsty and motionless.

## 003.jpg

stanza: 3 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-3.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#3
Krishna and gopas searched for the cows and reached the Munja forest. The Cows who lost their way were standing in distress. As Krishna went near them, a huge forest fire broke surrounding everyone faster.

## 004.jpg

stanza: 4 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-4.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#4
The fire was blazing in all directions making huge sounds and obstructing every possible way to escape. The gopas and cows were tormented by the fire and felt as if they were half burnt. They took shelter in Krishna, the only friend and saviour of all sorrows and cried to Him to save them from the fire.

## 005.jpg

stanza: 5 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-5.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#5
Krishna asked the gopas to close their eyes and when they opened after some time, they were back in Bhaandeera forest under the same banyan tree.

## 006.jpg

stanza: 6 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-6.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#6
Gopas praised Krishna for his glory which He accepted with a gracious smile. Krishna continued to sport with the gopas making them happy in the forest blooming with Paatala flowers indicating it is summer season.

## 007.jpg

stanza: 7 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-7.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#7
The intense heat of the summer time was like the weight of the heat of grief carried by people in their minds who are indifferent to Bhagwan. It was also like the heat which is created by worshipping Bhagwan, which dries up all the mud and slush of devotees. It was like the brilliance flowing from the chakra in Krishna’s hands. Krishna spent the rest of the summer season on the banks of Yamuna.

## 008.jpg

stanza: 8 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-8.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#8
The rainy season came where in the clouds resembled Krishna's body and the lightening was like His beautiful yellow garment. Krishna along with the gopas and cows spent the rainy season in the caves of the mountain Govardhana very happily.

## 009.jpg

stanza: 9 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-9.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#9
The mountain king Govardhana extended its hospitality by singing hymns of praise to Krishna, in the form of cooings of peacocks. The mountain worshipped Krishna by offering bloomed Jasmine and Kadamba flowers which fell from branches at His feet.

## 010.jpg

stanza: 10 | facebook file: D058/D058-2026-11-26-10.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#10
Then the spring season arrived with streams full of pure water, like the pure minds of Krishna’s devotees. Krishna enjoyed the spring season allowing the cows to graze in the fertile grasslands. Bhattathiri prayed to Lord Guruvayoorappan to cure him of his afflictions and bless with good health.
