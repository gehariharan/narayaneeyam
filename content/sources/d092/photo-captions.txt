#1
Vedas have described actions, which have to be done without expecting results. Bhattadiri surrenders all his actions and their results to Bhagwan’s lotus feet and attain freedom from action. He did not wish to do any action which is not listed in the vedas by his mind, intellect or speech. If by mistake, he committed any action not mentioned in vedas, he would surrender that karma also to Bhagwan.

## 002.jpg

stanza: 2 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-2.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#2
Bhattathiri wished to pray Bhagwan by invoking in his heart as a form of Vigraha made of clay, sand or any material and worship with flowers, sandalwood and perfumes, which are affordable to him.

## 003.jpg

stanza: 3 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-3.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#3
If women and people of lower varna are not privileged to hear to Bhagwan’s glories, they are still pitied. Bhattathiri says he is only worried about those people who were privileged to worship and yet are not peaceful with themselves. He prays to Bhagwan that he should not be like those privileged ones who perform Yajnas for material needs but not devoted to Bhagwan, turn proud and arrogant due to their fame, obtained out of their karmas.

## 004.jpg

stanza: 4 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-4.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#4
The undevoted people laugh at the devotees and say that these devotees, in order to hide their misconduct, are chanting names like Rama and Krishna. They go to the extent of calling the devoted people as hypocrites and laugh at them for worshipping Bhagwan always. Bhattathiri prayed to Bhagwan that never should he become like those undevoted people.

## 005.jpg

stanza: 5 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-5.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#5
People worshipped Bhagwan in white colour and pleased him through their meditation in Krita Yuga.
In Treta Yuga, Bhagwan was worshipped with sruka and sruva appearing in red colour like an yagna purusha.
In Dwapara Yuga, Bhagwan was worshipped with chakra and mace and with a dark coloured complexion.
In Kali Yuga, Bhagwan who is blue in complexion is worshipped through Nama sankeerthanam.

## 006.jpg

stanza: 6 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-6.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#6
The Kali is better than other yugas, because with small effort through the ways of Namasankeerthanam, one can attain Moksham. Therefore people who were born in Treta, Krita & Dwapara yuga would like to be born in Kali. Bhattathiri requested Bhagwan to help the people including him born in Kali to be not attracted to material desires.

## 007.jpg

stanza: 7 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-7.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#7
In this Kali, people are more devoted to Bhagwan and they will be mostly living in the Dravidian land. In that too, they would live along the banks of Kaveri, Tamiraparani, Vaigai and the banks of those rivers flowing towards west. Bhattathiri requested to be born in such regions and be devoted to Bhagwan all the time.

## 008.jpg

stanza: 8 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-8.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#8
Once king Pareekshit took his sword to kill Kali who is the enemy of Dharma (righteousness). But he did not kill Kali, because Kali knew the essence of Truth, and also as it has some good qualities.Only in Kali, service to God gives the best benefit sooner than even the sins. Even Kali is scared to afflict God's devotees. Only, before one turns to be God's devotee, he perhaps afflict them with diseases and only for that sake, Bhattathiri requested Bhagwan to punish the Kali.

## 009.jpg

stanza: 9 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-9.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#9
In this Kali Yuga, Gangaa, Geetaa, Gaayatri, Tulsika, Gopichandana, Saalagraama worship, Ekaadashi fast and chanting of Bhagwan names, these eight, the sages say, are quick and effortless means of salvation. Hence Bhattathiri requested Bhagwan to make him intensely interested in these eight things.

## 010.jpg

stanza: 10 | facebook file: D092/D092-2026-11-26-10.jpg | posted 2026-11-26 by Visalakshi Selvaraj

#10
If one abandons all actions and take a total surrender at Bhagwan's feet, he becomes relieved from any debt towards Gods, Sages, Pitrus and is no longer a servant to them. Bhagwan resides in that person’s heart and removes all his sins. Bhattathiri requested Bhagwan to remove all his afflictions caused due to sins committed by him.
