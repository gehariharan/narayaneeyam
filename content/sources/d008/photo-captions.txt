Dasakam 8

#1
At the end of the Prakriti Pralaya, in the first Kalpa which is known as Brahma Kalpa, Brahma came into existence. He got the knowledge of the Vedas from Bhagwan alone and began creation in the same way as in the previous Kalpas.

#2
One thousand Chaturyugaas make one day for this Brahma, and an equal duration is his night. During his day time he creates and at night he sleeps. His night is called the Naimittika Pralaya, when along with his creation he merges in Bhagwan. Thus he spent many days and nights.

#3
Brahma, by Bhagwan’s grace performs creation every day just as humans like us wake up in the morning and start our daily duties. For those who were born before the Brahma Kalpa, and for the eternal souls, being born in the new Kalpa is like getting up from sleep.

#4
Bhattathiri highlighted that  Brahma has completed 50 such cycles in life so far and started to explain the Bhagwan’s leela which happened from the time between Brahma's night to beginning of his next day.

#5
At the end of a creative day of Brahma, desirous of sleep, he along with the world merged in Bhagwan’s abdomen in subtle form. At that time the whole universe became one big ocean.

#6
At the time when the whole universe was reduced to causal waters, Bhagwan reclined on Aadi Shesha, the king of serpents who is also one of Bhagwan’s manifestations. Bhagwan were absorbed in yoga nidraa, in a state of pure bliss.

#7
The supreme blissful Bhagwan rested in yoga nidra on Adisheshan at the beginning of the Pralaya with all the various energies and the jeevas merged within, after directing time or Kaala to wake HIM up at the end of Pralaya.

#8
When thus during the Naimittika Pralaya consisting of a thousand chaturyugas, Bhagwan had slept, the power of time (Kaala Shakti) woke up first and awakened Bhagwan from the deep sleep.

#9
Bhagwan who were reclining in the causal waters, on waking up saw all the worlds merged in HIMSELF in subtle form. Bhagwan then cast a glance on all of them.

#10
There emerged from Bhagwan’s naval hole a wonderful divine lotus in the form of a bud, containing in subtle form all the worlds that remained merged in HIMSELF.

#11
That divine lotus bud, which originated from Bhagwan’s body, rose above the causal waters and with its brilliance, removed the surrounding darkness completely.

#12
From the petals of the Lotus, with help of yogic powers of Bhagwan, rose the Padmajan or Brahma who acquired knowledge from Vedas with grace of Lord and started creating on earth once again. 

#13
Bhattathiri prayed to the Supreme Bhagwan of incomprehensible powers and creator of Brahma in the present Padma kalpa, the Guruvayoorappan to eradicate all his ailments. Bhagwan nodded his head and acknowledged his request immediately.
